Signature: the active brief, case or campaign becomes a document on the desk, with waiting context and writing tools kept nearby.

How it works and what it costs

Wins: sustained copy, preparation and planning.

How it works and what it costs

Home: decision within the work register.
Inbox: inline case expansion.

Editor: two-email comparison.

Wins: fast scanning and operational comparison.
Risk: less warmth; rules must not overpower content.

Original reference study: technical document indices and operations registers. The date is a date, not a decorative performance score.

What to judge

Which working model feels right for your day? Open the brief, switch sequence steps, preview copy, search the writing library, and expand an Inbox case. Then compare the same screen at tablet and phone widths.

I’d shortlist Relay for execution and Folio for sustained writing. Index is the clearest density test.
