Compass needs a clearer centre of gravity.

The functional core is strong: a daily brief, triage, campaign preparation and delivery records. The interface gives similar weight to the next decision, background metrics, and work that is waiting on somebody else. The redesign should make the decision path visible before the operator has to assemble it.

  • Do next and Waiting need separate homes. A blocked Roof Safety task currently appears under highest leverage.
  • Counts need a scope. Inbox queue totals and Home’s waiting-reply snapshot describe different things.
  • Writing needs a focal point. The current component library and optional fields compete with the email itself.
  • Navigation should follow the day. Today, Growth, Delivery and Workspace organise retained capabilities.

Recommendation / Meridian

Compass has to move between judgement, writing, triage and structured records. Meridian handles that range most naturally. Keep the navy shell, warm work surfaces and decision hierarchy as one coherent direction; approve its Home reference before extending it to other screens.
