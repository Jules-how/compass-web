# Outbound tab (Pathway A)

One Sales tab. Calendar keeps the page. Inventory sits in a rail. Craft is a drill-in.

## Land

`/sales/outbound` is the Campaign Planner calendar. Nav item is Outbound. `/sales/pipeline` redirects here. Campaign pages stay at `/sales/pipeline/:id`.

## Calendar

Full height, same week / month / day grains. Chips show live vs blocked (missing leads, copy, or Instantly bind). Click still opens campaign review (leads + openers).

## Inventory rail (calendar view)

Left of the grid.

- **Next slots**: ranked recommendations. Promotable 90-day batches first, then fresh runway with at least one wave. Skip trades already dated this week. Fill toward 3–5 launches, never past 5.
- **Ready to recontact**: remaining 90-day batches (≥30) as gray cards.

Drag a card onto a day to date it. Recontact attaches the cohort. Fresh creates a planned campaign with that vertical. Click places on the next open weekday slot.

## Inspector

Review modal and campaign page both show four facts at the top: offer, leads on the campaign, openers written, Instantly bind. CRM links for attached vs ready-to-add.

## Craft

`/sales/outbound/craft` is the old hub minus the week queue (workshop, live Instantly, experiments, history, library). Planner toolbar has Craft.

## Not in this pass

Quarter / half-year / year planner grains. Split-screen calendar. Agent prose on the page. Attaching fresh sendable leads on drop (that stays a campaign-page action).
