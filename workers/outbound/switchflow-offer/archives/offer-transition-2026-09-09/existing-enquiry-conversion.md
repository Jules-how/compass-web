## Research specification — clarified 8 September 2026

Research fit is not sales qualification. A business can enter a diagnostic campaign from published relevance; an actual booking gap, eligible volume, available capacity and economic case must still be established before selling the service. Unknown pain must never become a personalised claim.

- Geography: Greater Sydney service coverage, including businesses labelled by suburb. Check the published service area, not just whether the city field literally says Sydney. Newcastle, Central Coast and Wollongong remain separate potential cells; do not silently widen this one.
- Business: an independent residential ducted installation/replacement operator with a direct homeowner enquiry and quote journey. Mixed residential/commercial firms may qualify if the residential service is explicit. Exclude commercial-only tenders, refrigeration-only repair, suppliers/manufacturers, directories and duct-cleaning-only businesses.
- Size: prioritise an established owner/manager-led operator with installation staff or an office/estimating function. Record a published team size if available; no hard employee or review-count cutoff. Multiple crews are a relevance clue, not proof of spare capacity, budget or enquiry volume.
- Required before contact: source-backed service and service-area evidence; a published relevant work email; checked identity/parent/domain overlap and outreach exclusions; the applicable contact basis; a recorded verification result under the existing policy. Keep missing and uncertain evidence on hold rather than deleting the company.
- Optional research: advertised installation promotions, recent installation project examples, dedicated quote form, finance offers, named estimator/office contact, published booking process. These guide questions. Do not infer active ads from a tracking pixel, lost enquiries from a form, or customer demand from review totals.
- Counterevidence: same-day quotes, instant pricing, a staffed booking process or a positive owner account of follow-up may mean the problem is elsewhere. Do not tell such a business it is missing calls. Ask where suitable enquiries stall, if anywhere.

First campaign: one exploratory angle around following up existing ducted-installation quote enquiries, qualifying homeowners, booking the right next step and reminders. No ads bundle, engineering/sizing advice, job guarantee, proof claim or simultaneous CTA/offer/location experiment. Lead with the workflow and a diagnostic question; do not promise incremental wins before evidence exists.

Compass owns the live relevance facts, campaign copy, lead statuses and preparation records. The local runner now reads that contract from Compass and keeps the existing tested processing engine. A queue request is not a hosted execution service. Changes to targeting or copy require another review of the prepared output.
