# Electrical Vertical Spec

Trade flag: `--trade electrical` `--trade electrician`
Campaign trade noun: `electrical`
Identity: `electri`

## 1. Maps query
- **Search terms** (city goes in `locationQueries`, never in the term): `electrical contractors`
- **Maps Categories**: `electrical contractor`, `electrician`

## 2. Specialty Target Keywords (Scraper & Opener)
- `switchboards`, `fault finding`, `power outage`, `safety switch`, `EV charger`, `smoke alarms`, `lighting`, `rewiring`

## 3. Qualification & High-Ticket Book-Now Focus
- **Pass (Book-Now)**: Switchboard upgrades, EV chargers, after-hours power faults. Quote from their named GP × extras.
- **Walk**: Commercial fitout tenders, industrial high-voltage BD, government infrastructure contractors. Search on ~$300 fault-call-only when cost per showed sits on or above GP. Cafe if carry is too thin.
- **Mail**: `{job_type}` = switchboard upgrades. Omit `{extra_jobs}`. Mix from switchboard GP or walk cafe. Do not mail a $300 call count.

## 4. Licensing & Script Handoff Boundaries
- Never provide technical electrical diagnosis or safety compliance advice over the bot.
- Capture emergency status, whether switchboard is sparking or smoking (instruct human immediate call), book standard residential callout.

## 5. Anti-ICP Walks Specific to Electrical
- Pure solar-only sales operations with no standard residential electrical service.
- Commercial data cabling / telecommunications only infrastructure firms.
- Service-call-only shops whose typical ticket is a ~$300 fault, if they want the guide or Search fill. Capture at a retainer under carry is still a mix.

## 6. Shop-sign tails
Not specialties. Strip from the trading name only.
- Words: `electrical`, `electricians`, `electrician`
- Phrases: `Electrical Services`, `Electrical Contractors`, `Electrical Contractor`
