# Roofing Vertical Spec

Trade flag: `--trade roofing` `--trade roofer`
Campaign trade noun: `roofing` / `roof repairs`
Identity: `\broof`

## 1. Maps query
- **Search terms** (city goes in `locationQueries`, never in the term): `roofing contractors`
- **Maps Categories**: `roofing contractor`, `roof repair shop`, `roofing supply store` (filter supply out).

## 2. Specialty Target Keywords (Scraper & Opener)
- `roof leak`, `storm damage`, `gutters`, `guttering`, `roof restoration`, `metal roofing`, `tile repairs`, `downpipes`, `fascia`

## 3. Qualification & High-Ticket Book-Now Focus
- **Pass (Book-Now)**: Storm leaks. Restore / re-roof mix can take the guide. Leak-only quotes ~carry ($1,200–$1,600 on 6–8 leaks at 50% of $400).
- **Walk**: Large insurance builder tenders, commercial cladding contractors, whole-subdivision new-build estimating firms. Search on leak patches when cost per showed sits on or above GP. Cafe if carry is too thin.
- **Mail**: `{job_type}` = roof leak repairs. Omit `{extra_jobs}`. Mix down on leak-only; do not mail 6–8 as if it covers the guide.

## 4. Licensing & Script Handoff Boundaries
- Never attempt to quote whole roof replacement costs or structural timber repairs over the bot.
- Gather leak location, roof type (tile, Colorbond/metal, slate), and urgency, then book inspection or hand to human dispatcher.

## 5. Anti-ICP Walks Specific to Roofing
- Pure solar panel installation companies with no general roof repair services.
- Building estimators, commercial civil cladding, scaffolding rental companies.
- Leak-patch only if they want the guide retainer. 6–8 leaks carry about $1,200–$1,600, not $2,497. Quote ~carry or walk cafe.

## 6. Shop-sign tails
Not specialties. Strip from the trading name only.
- Words: `roofing`, `roofers`, `roofer`
- Phrases: `Roofing Services`, `Roofing Contractors`, `Roofing Contractor`
