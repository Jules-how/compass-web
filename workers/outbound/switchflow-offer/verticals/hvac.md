# HVAC installation targeting

Current offer: installation-booking. Historical breakdown specification is archived under archives/offer-transition-2026-09-08/. This file supplies trade vocabulary; Compass offer relevance and campaign geography govern qualification.

Trade flag: `--trade hvac`  
Campaign trade noun: `aircon` / `HVAC`
Identity: `hvac|aircon|air conditioning|refrigerat`

## 1. Maps query
- **Search terms** (city goes in `locationQueries`, never in the term): `air conditioning contractor`, `air conditioning installation`, `ducted air conditioning`, `split system installation`, `HVAC contractor`, `heating and cooling contractor`, `air conditioning electrician`
- **Maps Categories**: `air conditioning contractor`, `hvac contractor`, `air conditioning repair service`, `electrician`
- Discovery is deliberately broad. These terms produce the candidate pool; website research supplies the installation-fit decision. Do not require the words `ducted`, `residential` or `installation` to appear in the listing name or category.

## 2. Specialty Target Keywords (Scraper & Opener)
Only use a service actually published by the business. Installation context first; a generic category alone is insufficient to qualify for the campaign.
- `ducted air conditioning`, `ducted installation`, `ducted`, `air conditioning installation`, `air conditioning replacement`, `split system installation`, `air conditioning`, `split system`, `heating`, `ventilation`

Mixed-service vocabulary preserves factual openers for existing records; it does not establish eligibility for the installation campaign. On newly researched installation rows, put the evidenced installation service first in the specialty/services fields.

## 3. Qualification
- Candidate for research: a real operating business listing or website in the target area with an AC, HVAC, electrical or plumbing service signal. Discovery does not require a named owner, employee estimate, revenue, review count, residential wording or a quote journey.
- Fit after research: published evidence that the business installs or replaces an eligible ducted reverse-cycle, multi-split/multi-head, multiple split-system package or ordinary split-system AC in the target area. Residential, commercial and mixed work all qualify.
- Hold for review: missing essential installation, operating-business or service-area evidence, uncertain identity, or a source that is too thin to decide. Missing optional contact or business-size data is retained as an information gap.
- A sophisticated booking process is not required for fit and is not an exclusion for ads plus booking.
- Call qualification: eligible volume, recoverable booking gap, capacity, job contribution and decision authority. Team size and reviews cannot establish these.

## 4. Delivery boundaries
- Booking and qualification are administrative. Technical assessment, sizing, installation advice and final quotes belong to the client's qualified staff.
- The base offer is ads plus booking. Never promise breakdown dispatch, guaranteed extra jobs, a refund guarantee or unapproved commercial terms.

## 5. Anti-ICP
- Refrigeration-only repair shops, parts wholesalers, duct-cleaning-only firms, national manufacturers and directories are not this buyer.
- Commercial-only or tender-focused contractors may be lower priority and need sales qualification, but are not automatically excluded.
- Preserve excluded and uncertain companies with a reason. Do not infer no residential work from one commercial page when other evidence is missing.

## 6. Shop-sign tails
Not specialties. Strip from the trading name only. `heating and cooling` lives here because it is on a lot of shop signs.
- Words: `hvac`, `aircon`
- Phrases: `Heating and Air Conditioning`, `Heating & Air Conditioning`, `Heating and Cooling`, `Heating & Cooling`, `Refrigeration`, `Air-Conditioning`, `Airconditioning`, `Aircoditioning`, `Conditioning`, `Mechanical Services`
