## 1. Maps query
- **Search terms** (city goes in `locationQueries`, never in the term): `locksmiths`
- **Maps Categories**: `locksmith`, `safe & vault shop`, `key duplication service`

## 2. Specialty Target Keywords (Scraper & Opener)
- `emergency locksmith service` (Maps category; opener may say emergency lockouts. Mail unit is after-hours lockouts or car keys.)
- `emergency lockout`, `lockout`, `rekey`, `lock rekeying`, `car keys`, `auto locksmith`, `digital locks`

## 3. Qualification & High-Ticket Book-Now Focus
- **Pass (Book-Now)**: After-hours lockouts, car keys, post-break-in replacement, urgent rekeying. Quote from carry on that mix. Search only if Planner cost per showed < named GP.
- **Walk**: Search on daytime lockouts (cost per showed sits on or above GP). Cafe economics if carry is too thin. Small shoe-repair kiosk key cutters with no mobile callout vans.
- **Mail**: `{job_type}` = after-hours lockouts or car keys. Omit `{extra_jobs}`. Mix capture under carry. Do not sell Search on the mid lockout.

## 4. Licensing & Script Handoff Boundaries
- Verification of property access identity rules.
- Fast dispatch with accurate address/vehicle make & model capture.

## 5. Anti-ICP Walks Specific to Locksmith
- Hardware store key cutting desks (Bunnings, Mister Minit).
- Static retail-only safe stores with no mobile dispatch van.
- Daytime lockout as Search fill (typical job ~$230; cost per showed sits on or above GP). Capture at a retainer under carry is still a mix.
