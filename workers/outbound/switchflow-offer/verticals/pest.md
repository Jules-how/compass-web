# Pest Control Vertical Spec

Trade flag: `--trade pest`  
Campaign trade noun: `pest control`
Identity: `\b(pest|termite)`

## 1. Maps query
- **Search terms** (city goes in `locationQueries`, never in the term): `pest control services`
- **Maps Categories**: `pest control service`

## 2. Specialty Target Keywords (Scraper & Opener)
- `termites`, `termite inspection`, `rodents`, `rats`, `spiders`, `cockroaches`, `possum removal`, `termite treatment`

## 3. Qualification & High-Ticket Book-Now Focus
- **Pass (Book-Now)**: Termite inspections and treatments in the mix. Quote from that GP. Spray-only quotes under carry or walks cafe.
- **Walk**: National corporate facilities pest contracts, agricultural crop spraying firms. Search on general spray when cost per showed sits on or above GP.
- **Mail**: `{job_type}` = termite inspections. Omit `{extra_jobs}`. Do not mail a barrier count. Mix from termite GP.

## 4. Licensing & Script Handoff Boundaries
- Script captures property size, pest type, and inspection access.
- Avoid quoting specialized structural chemical barrier treatments without an on-site technician inspection.

## 5. Anti-ICP Walks Specific to Pest Control
- National franchise networks (Jim's Pest Control, Flick Anticimex, Rentokil).
- DIY pest supply retailers.
- General-spray-only shops if they want the guide or Search fill. A ~$300 treatment cannot carry $2,497. Capture at a retainer under carry is still a mix.

## 6. Shop-sign tails
Not specialties. Strip from the trading name only.
- Words: `pest`
- Phrases: `Pest Control`, `Pest Management`
